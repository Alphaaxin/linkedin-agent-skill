# voice.md

Copy this to `~/.claude/linkedin/voice.md` and fill it in. Every skill in the
pack reads it. Ten minutes here is the difference between drafts you post and
drafts you rewrite.

The easiest way is `/li-start`: it asks a few questions and fills this in for
you. Or paste three of your own posts into Claude and say "write my voice.md
from these".
